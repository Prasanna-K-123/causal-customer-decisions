What the experiment supports

The learned candidate did not establish an improvement over the uniform men's email campaign. Selection was frozen before evaluating the final sample. The study preserves that result instead of optimizing against the test set.

“Men's” and “women's” describe campaign content. Customer gender is not observed. Financial contribution uses assumed margin and contact cost; it is not a measured profit figure.

How to challenge the result

Inspect the experiment and evaluation

Training, development and evaluation sets are separate. Assignment probabilities are known. A cross-fitted doubly robust learner competes with uniform campaigns and a transparent purchase-segment rule. The primary policy contrast uses paired customer scores and a 2,000-resample bootstrap.

Understand the score

Gamma_a = mu_a(X) + I(A=a) × (Y − mu_a(X)) / p_a

For the chosen action, scenario value is margin times the estimated spending score, less contact cost. Correct randomization supports value estimation even if the spending regression is imperfect.

Check uncertainty and transport limits

99.14% of the evaluation customers spent nothing. Sparse purchases and heavy tails limit precision. Intervals condition on the trained policies; they do not include variation from retraining on another experiment. Historical results do not establish current Indian campaign performance. The scenario grid is exploratory, with pointwise intervals rather than simultaneous confidence bands.
